# Candidates — Rename the Planet

Brief (from RULES.txt): one line under 200 characters that gives a
planet of our solar system a funny new name and says why in the same
breath. The house judge compares lines pairwise on, in order:
(1) names a solar-system planet with a funny new name plus the why,
(2) is funny on first read, the laugh coming from the idea rather
than a pun on the planet's real name alone, (3) the why earns the
name by leaning on that planet's own traits so it would not fit any
other planet, (4) is fresh, not the obvious pun anyone would reach
for, (5) is tight, every word doing work.

Stock angles deliberately avoided: Uranus anatomy puns, Saturn as a
"Lord of the Rings" riff, Mars renamed for redness alone, Earth as a
"pale blue dot" quote.

## The 16 candidates

1. **Earth:** Call Earth 'Dirt': it's 71% ocean, but the only species
   that got a vote named it after the dry part it stands on.
2. **Saturn:** Call Saturn 'The Floater': it's less dense than water,
   so in a big enough bathtub the whole ringed giant would bob.
3. **Jupiter:** Jupiter is now 'The Group Chat Admin': a 10-hour day,
   95 moons reading along, and one storm that never stops typing.
4. **Neptune:** Neptune is now 'Math's Revenge': found by equations
   before any telescope saw it, it has blown 2,100 km/h winds at the
   doubters ever since.
5. **Uranus:** Call Uranus 'The Cold Shoulder': the coldest planet at
   -224C, tipped on its side, facing away from everyone 21 years at
   a time.
6. **Venus:** Venus is now 'The Sauna With No Exit': 465C, acid rain,
   and a day longer than its year, so leaving takes forever anyway.
7. **Mercury:** Call Mercury 'Leftovers': baked at 430C by day, frozen
   at -180C by night, with no atmosphere to keep either temperature.
8. **Mars:** Mars is now 'The Group Project': two potato-shaped moons,
   a volcano the size of France, and no atmosphere to show for it.
9. **Earth:** Earth is now 'The Only One With Tenants': every other
   planet sits vacant; this one has eight billion residents and a
   noise complaint.
10. **Neptune:** Neptune is now 'The One-Lap Wonder': found in 1846,
    it finished its first full orbit only in 2011, into 2,100 km/h
    headwinds.
11. **Saturn:** Saturn is now 'Engagement Season': it wears a ring
    280,000 km wide made of ice chunks, and still nobody has
    proposed.
12. **Jupiter:** Call Jupiter 'The Bouncer': 1,300 Earths fit inside,
    95 moons in its entourage, and a red storm older than telescopes.
13. **Venus:** Venus is now 'Passive-Aggressive': it spins backwards,
    slowly, under a blanket of acid, smiling the whole time.
14. **Mercury:** Mercury is now 'The Deadline': its year lasts 88
    days but one day takes 176, so even its calendar gives up
    halfway.
15. **Mars:** Mars is now 'The Abandoned Mall': dried-up rivers, dead
    volcanoes, and two lumpy moons nobody ever claimed.
16. **Uranus:** Uranus is now 'The Sideways Nap': tipped 98 degrees,
    it rolls around the sun like it fell asleep mid-orbit and never
    got up.

## Local judging

Scored against the five criteria, most important first.

- **#1 Earth 'Dirt'** — Funny on first read: the joke is an idea, not
  a name pun; a mostly-ocean planet named after dirt by the one
  species standing on the dry bit. The why could fit no other planet
  (71% ocean plus the only naming species). Fresh: it turns the
  renaming task on ourselves instead of reaching for a planet pun.
  Tight: one setup clause, one punch, no wasted words. **Winner.**
- **#2 Saturn 'The Floater'** — Specific density fact and a good
  bathtub image, but part of the laugh rides on the word "floater"
  itself, and the density fact is the one Saturn trivia line many
  entrants will also reach for. Runner-up.
- **#3 Jupiter 'Group Chat Admin'** — Three real traits (10-hour day,
  95 moons, Great Red Spot) and a fresh metaphor, but "reading along"
  stretches the moons into the metaphor and the line runs long.
- **#4 Neptune 'Math's Revenge'** — Discovery-by-equations is
  Neptune-only and fresh, but "at the doubters" invents an audience
  the planet never had; the punch is softer than #1.
- **#5 Uranus 'Cold Shoulder'** — Coldest-planet fact plus the 98
  degree tilt plus 21-year seasons is a strong trait stack, but three
  facts in one line crowds the joke.
- **#6 Venus 'Sauna With No Exit'** — Heat, acid and day-longer-than-
  year all earn the name; the sauna image is a touch familiar.
- **#7 Mercury 'Leftovers'** — The 430C/-180C swing with no atmosphere
  is Mercury-specific, but "leftovers" lands as a label more than a
  joke.
- **#8 Mars 'Group Project'** — Potato moons and a France-sized
  volcano are nicely specific; the group-project punch ("no
  atmosphere to show for it") is decent but broader than #1.
- **#9 Earth 'Tenants'** — Funny and Earth-only, but "noise
  complaint" is a stock tag and the line is longer than #1 for the
  same planet.
- **#10 Neptune 'One-Lap Wonder'** — The 1846-to-2011 single orbit is
  a lovely fact, but the name mostly restates the why.
- **#11 Saturn 'Engagement Season'** — Cute, but the ring-as-jewellery
  angle is the obvious Saturn reach the brief warns against.
- **#12 Jupiter 'Bouncer'** — Size and moons earn it, yet "entourage"
  and the storm make three images compete.
- **#13 Venus 'Passive-Aggressive'** — Backwards slow spin fits the
  name, but "smiling the whole time" adds nothing the acid blanket
  did not already say.
- **#14 Mercury 'Deadline'** — Day-longer-than-year is a great trait;
  "calendar gives up" is a weaker punch than the fact deserves.
- **#15 Mars 'Abandoned Mall'** — Evocative, but the why lists scenery
  rather than making the name inevitable.
- **#16 Uranus 'Sideways Nap'** — Clean tilt joke with no stock pun,
  but one trait only, and #5 uses the same planet harder.

## Winner

`Call Earth 'Dirt': it's 71% ocean, but the only species that got a vote named it after the dry part it stands on.`

It is the only candidate that is simultaneously an idea-joke (not a
planet-name pun), impossible to move to another planet, fresh
against a field of planet puns, and tight enough that every word
works. Written into `main.py`.
